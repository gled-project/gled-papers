# GLED – an Implementation of a Hierarchic Server–Client Model

Matevž Tadel. Published as a chapter of *Applied Parallel and Distributed
Computing*, edited by Y. Pan and L. T. Yang, Advances in Computation:
Theory and Practice, volume 16, Nova Science Publishers, 2005,
ISBN 1-59454-174-4.

The sources here are the author's submitted version, the preprint, which
Nova Science Publishers permits authors to self-archive. Cite the published
chapter. The copyright of the published chapter is held by Nova Science
Publishers, so these files are not under the licenses of the rest of this
repository.

`gled-ihscm-1.2.0.tex` is the version prepared for the book in 2003, set in
the SIAM class `siamltex` and with keywords, as the editors asked of the
authors. `gled-ihscm.tex` is the paper in the LaTeX article class. Both
include the performance measurements of `perf.texf`. The figures are in
`figs/`, as xfig sources with EPS and PNG renderings.
